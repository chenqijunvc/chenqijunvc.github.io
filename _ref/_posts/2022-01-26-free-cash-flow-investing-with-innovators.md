Free cash flow analysis reveals the extent to which companies that invest heavily in R&D outperform their peers, suggesting investors should be looking to ensure these innovators form a core part of their portfolios going forward.

**Key Insights:**

- Companies combining high R&D intensity with strong free cash flow generation tend to outperform
- FCF analysis helps distinguish between genuine innovators and companies spending on R&D without profitable results
- The intersection of innovation and profitability provides a differentiated source of returns
- This research underpinned the development of the Abacus FCF Innovation Leaders ETF (ABOT), which applies a dual screen for FCF-ROIC and R&D intensity

The full paper examines the empirical evidence across multiple time periods and market conditions, demonstrating the robustness of combining free cash flow quality with innovation metrics.
