    <p class="lead">My career has been focused on one question: <strong>how do you build investment processes that consistently identify and capture value?</strong></p>

    <p>I approach this from three angles — as an <strong>architect</strong> who designs the research framework and portfolio construction methodology, as a <strong>portfolio manager</strong> who operates the process day to day, and as a <strong>researcher</strong> who develops the quantitative frameworks that drive decisions.</p>

    <p>Over roughly a decade at FCF Advisors / Abacus FCF Advisors (2017–2024), I designed and operated a quantitative investment platform from scratch — building the research infrastructure, portfolio management systems, and operational processes that supported five ETFs across U.S. large cap, real assets, innovation, small cap, and fixed income strategies.</p>

    <p>My intellectual contributions include the <strong>Pure Alpha framework</strong> for evaluating active management, and research into <strong>free cash flow</strong> as a measure of quality, profitability, and investment performance.</p>

    <p>This site is an archive of that work — organized around contributions rather than employers, designed to be durable across decades rather than updated quarterly.</p>
