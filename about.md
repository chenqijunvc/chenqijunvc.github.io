    <p class="lead">I build the engines that power modern active management.</p>

    <p>My career is the story of growing <em>with</em> a platform — from my first day as a Quantitative Analyst in 2017 through becoming an SEC-registered Portfolio Manager overseeing a five-ETF suite — and now stepping into the next chapter: active portfolio management reimagined for the AI era.</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>The FCF Chapter (2017–2025)</h3>

    <p>I joined FCF Advisors in October 2017 — not as a founder, but as an analyst with a mandate to build. Over eight years, I:</p>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>Built the quant research infrastructure</strong> from scratch — data pipelines, factor library, signal generation, backtesting frameworks, and automated reporting</li>
      <li><strong>Designed and operated portfolio construction</strong> processes across five active ETFs spanning U.S. large cap, real assets, innovation, small cap, and fixed income</li>
      <li><strong>Authored SEC prospectuses</strong> (N-1A filings) and regulatory documentation for new fund launches</li>
      <li><strong>Developed the Pure Alpha framework</strong> for active manager evaluation, and <strong>free cash flow research</strong> that redefined quality factor construction</li>
      <li><strong>Grew through every role</strong>: Quantitative Analyst &rarr; Senior Quant &rarr; Portfolio Manager &rarr; Director of Research &rarr; SEC-registered Portfolio Manager (2024)</li>
    </ul>

    <p>The platform I built now manages capital as part of Abacus Global Management, following the firm's acquisition by Abacus Life. I serve as VP of Public Equity &amp; Portfolio Management, ensuring continuity of the investment process I built.</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>The Transition</h3>

    <p>In 2024, I earned the CFA charter and was named as an SEC-registered portfolio manager — the institutional capstone of a career built from the ground up. But the industry was changing faster than ever.</p>

    <p>I made a deliberate decision: the next decade of active management belongs to those who can fuse quantitative discipline with machine intelligence. So I went back to school.</p>

    <p>I am now pursuing a <strong>Master of Science in Computer Science &amp; AI at Georgia Tech</strong> — not to become an engineer, but to bring modern AI capabilities into the active management toolkit.</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>Now</h3>

    <p>I speak at <strong>Future Alpha</strong> (2026 panelist, 2027 spotlight interview) on signal fusion and multi-factor investing. I build tools that bridge quantitative finance and modern AI. I write about the intersection of active management, factor investing, and machine intelligence.</p>

    <p>My goal is clear: to practice active portfolio management the way it should be done in the AI era — systematic, research-driven, and augmented by intelligence that scales beyond what a human can process alone.</p>
