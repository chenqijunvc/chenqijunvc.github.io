    <p class="lead">My career has centered on one question: <strong>how do you transform investment research into repeatable, institutional-grade investment decisions?</strong></p>

    <p>I approach this question from three angles — as an <strong>architect</strong> who designs the research frameworks and portfolio construction systems, as a <strong>portfolio manager</strong> who operates those systems day to day, and as a <strong>researcher</strong> who develops the quantitative methods that bridge data and decision.</p>

    <p>For roughly a decade, I designed and operated a quantitative investment platform at FCF Advisors / Abacus FCF Advisors — building the infrastructure, methodologies, and processes that turned research into portfolios across five ETFs spanning U.S. large cap, real assets, innovation, small cap, and fixed income.</p>

    <p>My intellectual contributions include the <strong>Pure Alpha framework</strong> for evaluating active management, and <strong>free cash flow research</strong> that redefines quality factor construction around cash generation rather than earnings.</p>

    <p>This site is organized around the work itself — the systems built, the research produced, and the professional record behind them — not around any single employer or role.</p>
