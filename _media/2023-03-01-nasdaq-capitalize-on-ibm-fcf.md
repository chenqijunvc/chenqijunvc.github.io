Nasdaq featured FCF Advisors' approach through the lens of IBM's growing free cash flow. Vince (Qijun) Chen, Director of Research and Portfolio Manager, was quoted: "At FCF Advisors we offer a unique way to gauge quality investment. We measure profitability with free cash flow instead of earnings, which is subject to manager discretion." The article discussed how IBM's free cash flow grew to $9.3 billion in 2022 (up $2.8 billion from 2021) and how TTAC provides exposure to companies with strong free cash flow generation through the FCF Quality Model's stock selection process.
