FCF Advisors was named to the PSN Top Guns List for Q3 2024. The FCF US Quality Strategy received Top Gun 3 Star Category awards (top ten returns over three years in the all-cap core universe), and the FCF US Small Cap Strategy received a 1 Star Category award (top ten returns for the quarter in small-cap core). Vince (Qijun) Chen, Portfolio Manager of both strategies, commented: "Earning the 3 Star Category Award for three consecutive years highlights the strategy's reliability as a versatile core investment for diverse portfolios. Additionally, the 1 Star Category Award for our FCF US Small Cap Strategy underscores the robustness of FCFQM in navigating small-cap markets."
