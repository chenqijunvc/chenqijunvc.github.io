ETF Trends covered the "When Markets Wobble, Cash Remains King: Free Cash Flow Investing" webcast featuring Bob Shea (CEO/CIO) and Vince (Qijun) Chen. Chen explained that stocks with strong free cash flow profitability outperform the market in almost every sector, arguing that earnings-based approaches suffer from accounting discretion. He detailed the Free Cash Flow Quality Model (FCFQM), which uses cash flow inputs rather than earnings to select companies with strong and sustainable profitability that outperform throughout the cycle. The webcast highlighted the FCF US Quality ETF (TTAC) and FCF International Quality ETF (TTAI) as vehicles accessing this methodology.
