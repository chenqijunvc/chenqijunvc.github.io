Vince Chen served as a panelist at Future Alpha 2026 on the Alpha X stage, joining quantitative leaders from Citi, ClearBridge Investments, and Fidelity Investments. The panel "Signal Fusion in Multi-Factor Models — Beyond Momentum and Mean Reversion" explored where investment edge comes from in an era of ubiquitous factor access. Chen brought the perspective of signal fusion methodology as a competitive moat, drawing from Abacus FCF Advisors' experience building the suite of free cash flow ETFs.
