Abacus FCF Advisors was named to the PSN Top Guns List for Q4 2025, earning the Manager of the Decade designation — awarded to strategies in the top ten performers over the latest 10-year period with an R-squared of 0.80+ relative to the style benchmark, returns greater than the benchmark, and lower standard deviation. Vince (Qijun) Chen, CFA, Portfolio Manager, commented: "The Manager of the Decade designation is a meaningful validation of that track record. Our process has delivered excess returns with less risk than the style benchmark across a full decade that included the pandemic, the most aggressive rate hiking cycle in 40 years, and the current AI-driven market." Chen also addressed the challenging second half of 2025 for quality investing, noting that "investing in companies that are generating durable free cash flow" offers a fundamentally different proposition from theme-driven approaches.
