    <p class="lead">From 2017 through 2026, I built and operated quantitative infrastructure that connected investment research to live ETF portfolios.</p>

    <h2>Research infrastructure</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Data pipelines</strong> — ingestion, cleaning, normalization, and quality control across financial datasets</li>
      <li><strong>Factor library</strong> — implementation and maintenance of investment signals and research definitions</li>
      <li><strong>Signal generation</strong> — combining factor inputs into portfolio-relevant scores</li>
      <li><strong>Backtesting</strong> — strategy simulation, diagnostics, and sensitivity analysis</li>
      <li><strong>Automation</strong> — repeatable workflows for research, computation, and reporting</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Portfolio-management infrastructure</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Portfolio construction</strong> — translating signals into target weights within portfolio constraints</li>
      <li><strong>Rebalancing</strong> — scheduled reconstitutions and between-cycle monitoring</li>
      <li><strong>Risk monitoring</strong> — position, sector, turnover, and tracking-risk oversight</li>
      <li><strong>Performance attribution</strong> — decomposing portfolio results and diagnosing drivers</li>
      <li><strong>Operational reporting</strong> — repeatable investment and client-reporting workflows</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>ETF and index operations</h2>
    <p>The role also included work across index methodologies, regulatory and fund documentation, reconstitution workflows, corporate actions, and coordination with the broader ETF operating ecosystem.</p>
