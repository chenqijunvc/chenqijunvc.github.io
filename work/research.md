    <p class="lead">My research focuses on quantitative methods for evaluating investment decisions — distinguishing skill from style, measuring quality through cash generation rather than earnings, and building systems that make research actionable.</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>Pure Alpha Framework</h3>
    <p>A systematic method for evaluating active management. Separates genuine manager skill from stylistic tailwinds through holdings-based factor decomposition.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Holdings-based factor decomposition — mapping every position to its factor exposures</li>
      <li>Return attribution — separating factor returns from active bets</li>
      <li>Consistency analysis — evaluating whether active returns are persistent or episodic</li>
      <li>Skill assessment — distinguishing stock-picking skill from factor timing</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Free Cash Flow Research</h3>
    <p>Research into free cash flow as a measure of quality, profitability, and investment performance. The core thesis: earnings are an opinion, cash flow is a fact.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>FCF-ROIC framework for identifying genuinely high-quality companies</li>
      <li>Four-signal quality model: FCF Margin, FCF ROIC, Accruals Ratio, Capital Allocation Discipline</li>
      <li>Applications across U.S. large cap, small cap, innovation, and fixed income</li>
    </ul>
