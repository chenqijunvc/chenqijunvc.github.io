<p class="lead">This 2026 working paper introduces a research framework for finding better fund alternatives by separating what an investment delivers from the value that remains after comparable exposures are accounted for.</p>

<h2>Research focus</h2>
<p>The work examines fund selection as an investable comparison problem: identify the exposures a fund provides, measure the return components that can be explained or replicated, and evaluate whether a more efficient alternative may exist.</p>
