    <p class="lead">From 2017 to 2024, I designed, built, and operated a quantitative investment platform at FCF Advisors (acquired by Abacus Life in 2024). This page documents what I built, my responsibilities, and the evidence supporting each claim.</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>Context</h3>
    <p>I joined FCF Advisors in 2017 — before the ETF platform existed, before the Abacus acquisition. The firm was a research shop without investment products. Over the next several years, I built the infrastructure, designed the methodologies, and operated the systems that became a multi-ETF institutional platform.</p>
    <p class="small text-muted">Tier 4</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>Research Infrastructure</h3>
    <p>I designed and implemented the firm's quantitative research platform from the ground up:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Data pipeline</strong> — ingesting, cleaning, and normalizing financial data from multiple sources</li>
      <li><strong>Factor library</strong> — implementing and maintaining the proprietary factor set</li>
      <li><strong>Signal generation</strong> — combining factors into composite signals</li>
      <li><strong>Backtesting framework</strong> — simulating strategy performance with realistic assumptions</li>
      <li><strong>Automation</strong> — eliminating manual research workflows through automated collection, computation, and report generation</li>
    </ul>
    <p>The research engine I built became the analytical foundation for all subsequent products.</p>
    <p class="small text-muted">Tier 4 (methodology proprietary — implementation detail not disclosed)</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>Portfolio Management</h3>
    <p>I designed and operated the portfolio construction process across all five ETFs:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Portfolio construction methodology</strong> — translating signals into portfolio weights, applied across all strategies</li>
      <li><strong>Risk management framework</strong> — position limits, sector constraints, turnover controls</li>
      <li><strong>Rebalancing engine</strong> — systematic reconstitution on scheduled cycles</li>
      <li><strong>Trade execution</strong> — from signal to executed trade with compliance checks</li>
      <li><strong>Cash management</strong> — inflows, outflows, dividend reinvestment</li>
    </ul>
    <p>I was the day-to-day portfolio manager for ABFL, ABLD, ABOT, ABLS, and ABXB from each fund's inception through 2024.</p>
    <p class="small text-muted">Tier 1 — Portfolio construction methodology referenced in prospectuses (SEC EDGAR)</p>

    <hr class="my-4">

    <h3>ETF Operations</h3>
    <p>I designed and managed the operational workflows that kept the ETFs running:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>NAV monitoring</strong> and oversight</li>
      <li><strong>Creation/redemption</strong> workflow coordination with market makers and APs</li>
      <li><strong>Regulatory reporting</strong> — N-CSR, N-PORT, N-CEN</li>
      <li><strong>Index administration</strong> — authored index methodologies, executed quarterly reconstitutions</li>
      <li><strong>SEC filing authoring</strong> — primary author of N-1A investment strategy sections, prospectuses</li>
      <li><strong>Collaboration</strong> with fund administrator, custodian, and external counsel</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="small text-muted">Tier 1 — SEC filings; Tier 3 — operational coordination records</p>
