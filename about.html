Portfolio Manager. Quantitative Researcher. Builder of Investment Platforms. Over nine years at Abacus FCF Advisors, I helped build and operate a quantitative asset management platform spanning research, portfolio management, ETFs, indices, and investment technology. I led the quantitative research function, portfolio management process, and development of investment technology.

Products

I contributed to the research, launch, and ongoing portfolio management of the firm's ETF product suite:

  • ABFL — FCF Leaders ETF (U.S. large cap)
  • ABLD — FCF Leaders Real Assets ETF
  • ABOT — FCF Leaders Innovation ETF
  • ABLS — FCF Leaders Small Cap ETF
  • ABXB — FCF Leaders Flexible Fixed Income ETF

Responsibilities

My day-to-day work spanned the full investment process:

  • Led quantitative research and model development
  • Led portfolio management and trade execution
  • Led ETF operations and index reconstitution
  • Led product development and SEC filings (N-1A, N-CSR, prospectuses)
  • Client presentations and institutional due diligence
  • Led research automation and investment technology
  • Investment committee participation

Public Record

Throughout my tenure, I represented the platform in public settings and published research:

  • SEC filings — registered as portfolio manager on multiple ETF prospectuses
  • Abacus Investor Day 2025 — featured speaker; CEO Jay Jackson introduced me as "that portfolio manager"
  • CBOE Global Markets interview — discussed the firm's investment approach
  • Published white papers and research articles on quantitative finance and investing
  • PSN Top Guns Manager of the Decade (Q4 2025) — long-term performance recognition

Tenure

I joined FCF Advisors in 2017 — before the Abacus acquisition, before the ETF platform existed. I held progressively senior roles in research and portfolio management. When Abacus Life acquired FCF Advisors in 2024, I continued as portfolio manager, helped integrate the platform, trained new team members, and managed the transition while maintaining day-to-day investment operations.

By 2026, the platform was operating with a complete ETF suite, established client relationships, and a functioning team — and I was ready for the next chapter.
